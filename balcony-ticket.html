The Passion Project

The Balcony Ticket

A running series of feel-good stories, filmed wherever I happen to be — usually my balcony. I say I'm bored, I show you whatever I'm doing, and somewhere in the middle, I end up telling you a story from my life.

How an episode actually goes

"I'm bored, so I'm doing this." Somewhere in the middle of doing it — a memory surfaces. By the end, you've watched me make tea, or reorganize a shelf, or just sit outside — and you've also heard a real story: about growing up, about people I've known, about the small feel-good moment that stuck.

No script, no production crew — just an ordinary moment used as a doorway into something true. It's the opposite of a highlight reel, which is exactly the point.
